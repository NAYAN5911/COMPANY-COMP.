# Company Law & ROC Compliance Templates (India)

Trackers, checklists and board templates for Companies Act, 2013 compliance: ROC annual calendar, board meeting tracker, directors/KMP register, and ready-to-edit resolutions, notices and minutes.

> Due dates, fees, forms and exemptions change through MCA circulars and amendments. Timelines in these files are the commonly cited statutory ones - **verify against the current Act, Rules and MCA notifications** before relying on them.
> All files contain **dummy data only**. Do not commit real DINs, CINs or company records.

## Contents
| Folder | What is inside |
|---|---|
| `compliance-trackers/` | ROC compliance calendar (formula-driven due dates and status), board meeting tracker (gap, notice, quorum checks), directors and KMP register |
| `checklists/` | Annual compliance, AGM, director appointment, incorporation |
| `board-templates/` | Notice and agenda of board meeting, board resolution for director appointment, AGM notice outline, minutes |
| `docs/` | How to use and publish |

## Quick start
1. Open an `.xlsx` file. Blue cells are inputs, black cells are formulas, yellow cells are key dates.
2. Overwrite rows marked EXAMPLE and fill in your company's FY end and AGM date.
3. Copy the Markdown templates into your own letterhead and tailor the bracketed fields.

## Disclaimer
